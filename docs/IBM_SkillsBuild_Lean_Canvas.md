## Detailed Block-by-Block Information for Form Filling

If you are using the online tool at [https://www.leanfoundry.com/tools/lean-canvas](https://www.leanfoundry.com/tools/lean-canvas), copy and paste each section below into its corresponding box:

### Box 1: Problem
1. **Lack of Personalized Career Direction:** In Tier-2/3 engineering colleges, student-to-counselor ratios exceed 1000:1, leaving students confused about industry-aligned paths.
2. **Curriculum-Industry Mismatch:** College syllabi lag behind real-world tech stacks, leaving graduates unprepared for hiring benchmarks.
3. **Black-Box Guidance:** Existing online platforms offer vague, generic advice behind expensive paywalls without actionable skill-gap analysis.
* **Existing Alternatives:** Static career blogs, costly 1-on-1 private career coaches, generic aptitude questionnaires.

### Box 2: Customer Segments
* **Primary Segment:** Undergraduate engineering & computer science students in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities.
* **Secondary Segment:** Fresh graduates and job seekers transitioning into technology roles.
* **Tertiary Segment:** College placement cells and training coordinators seeking cohort readiness metrics.
* **Early Adopters:** Final-year tech students actively seeking internships and campus placement direction.

### Box 3: Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
* *"Transparent, mathematical career alignment powered by hybrid AI vector matching and evidence-based confidence scoring."*
* **High-Level Concept:** A personalized career match engine that maps a student's exact skill DNA to 7 high-growth tech industry careers.

### Box 4: Solution
1. **Multi-Factor Assessment:** Captures 18 skills with 4-level proficiency ratings, 12 interest areas, and 33 technologies.
2. **Hybrid Matching Algorithm:** 63-dimensional vector cosine similarity (`scikit-learn`) + calibrated rule heuristics.
3. **Transparent Explanations:** Real-time AI Match percentage, Evidence Confidence tier (High, Moderate, Preliminary), and missing skill breakdowns.
4. **Actionable Roadmap:** Curated learning steps, priority technologies, and printable career reports.

### Box 5: Channels
1. **University Placement Portals:** Partnerships with institutional career and training cells.
2. **Student Developer Communities:** Student clubs, Google Developer Groups (GDG), hackathons.
3. **Digital & Social Presence:** Tech blogs, LinkedIn student showcases, open-source GitHub showcase.

### Box 6: Revenue Streams
1. **B2C Premium Tier:** Free initial assessment; premium detailed 90-day adaptive learning plans and resume reviews.
2. **B2B Institutional SaaS:** Annual subscription for colleges to track batch-wide skill readiness and placement analytics.
3. **EdTech Affiliations:** Verified course and certification referral commissions for bridging identified skill gaps.

### Box 7: Cost Structure
1. **Infrastructure:** Python/Flask web hosting and lightweight SQLite/PostgreSQL cloud database.
2. **Maintenance:** Continuous benchmark calibration reflecting emerging tech hiring trends.
3. **Operations:** Domain registration, SSL certificates, and platform monitoring.

### Box 8: Key Metrics
1. **Assessment Completion Rate:** Percentage of students completing the 4-part assessment.
2. **Recommendation Accuracy:** Student satisfaction and relevance ratings on recommended career paths.
3. **Actionability Metric:** Percentage of students who download/print the results and follow the learning roadmap.
4. **Cohort Growth:** Institutional adoption and recurring active student profiles.

### Box 9: Unfair Advantage
* **Deterministic & Explainable Hybrid Model:** Combines continuous vector geometry with rule validation—ensuring mathematically bounded, reproducible, and explainable recommendations without dependence on expensive proprietary black-box APIs.
* **Zero Overhead Architecture:** Works seamlessly on low-bandwidth connections and lightweight hardware.
